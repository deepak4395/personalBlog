## Explanation

### Context
This verse appears in the third chapter of the Bhagavad Gita, known as Karma Yoga, which focuses on the path of selfless action. In this chapter, Lord Krishna instructs Arjuna on the importance of performing one's duty without attachment to the fruits of those actions. This teaching is crucial as Arjuna is initially reluctant to engage in battle due to his emotional turmoil and attachment to his family. Krishna emphasizes that true fulfillment comes from acting in accordance with one's dharma (duty) while remaining detached from the outcomes.

### Deeper Understanding
In this verse, Krishna articulates a profound truth about the nature of selfless action. He teaches Arjuna that those who engage in the creation or action for the sake of the world, without seeking personal gain or attachment to the results, embody a state of peace. This state of being is contrasted with those who act with selfish motives, who often experience anxiety and suffering due to their attachments.

Krishna’s teachings here resonate with the broader themes of the Gita, particularly those of **dharma** (righteous duty) and **karma** (action). The underlying message is that actions performed with the right intent lead to spiritual growth and societal benefit. He emphasizes that one’s true essence is not found in personal ambition but in the collective well-being of all beings.

### Practical Application
1. **Selfless Service**: In our daily lives, we can practice selfless service by engaging in community service or volunteering without expecting recognition or reward. For instance, participating in local food drives or environmental clean-up efforts can help foster a sense of community and personal fulfillment.

2. **Work Ethic**: In professional settings, focusing on the quality of work rather than seeking promotions or accolades can lead to greater satisfaction and productivity. This means investing effort into your job and taking pride in your contributions, irrespective of the recognition received.

3. **Mindfulness in Action**: Embracing mindfulness allows individuals to remain focused on the task at hand rather than being consumed by the outcomes. Practicing mindfulness meditation can help cultivate this state of being, enabling one to act with clarity and purpose.
